If nothing vibrates at all

navigator.vibrate() returning true does not mean the phone actually buzzed — it only means the request was accepted. Android silently drops it in these cases:
  1. Touch feedback is off. Settings → Sound & vibration → Vibration & haptics → Touch feedback. If this is off or at zero, Chrome's web vibration is suppressed system-wide. This is the single most common cause.
  2. Ringer is on Silent. Silent (not Vibrate) suppresses haptics on most Android builds. Do Not Disturb can do the same.
  3. Battery saver disables vibration on many phones.
  4. Chrome site settings. Long-press the address bar → Site settings → check nothing is blocked for superfanshuffle.com.
  5. A cached old build. Check the version below matches what you expect.
